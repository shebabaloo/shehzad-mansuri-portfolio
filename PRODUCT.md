# Product Brief — The Living Score

## Product

An expressive personal-professional playground for Shehzad “Shez” Mansuri. It
combines an embedded resume, AI-native systems, personal experiments, and interests
into a cinematic scroll narrative.

## Primary Outcomes

Visitors should remember Shez, understand how he creatively uses AI to turn ambiguity
into systems, download his resume, and have an obvious path to contact him.

The site is not organized around formal case studies and should not read like a
recruiter dashboard.

## Audience

- People considering Shehzad for PM, TPM, AI, or product-building opportunities.
- Potential collaborators and people interested in his experiments.
- Friends and professional peers exploring his broader creative identity.

## Public Framing and Privacy

- Deloitte may be named.
- Client identity is **FAANG company**.
- Public organization labels are **AI Privacy** and **Infrastructure & Data Centers**.
- Never publish Meta, internal team names, or internal product names.
- Quantified professional claims require explicit verification before publication.

Formal resume and metadata use **Shehzad Mansuri**. The creative experience uses
**Shez**.

## Content Movements

1. Overture / Meet Shez
2. Work in Motion — embedded resume chronology
3. Systems That Compound — Second Brain, Team Third Brain, and this website
4. Off the Clock — volleyball, books, philosophy, games, clarinet, orchestral music,
   and jazz
5. Variations — emerging personal experiments
6. Coda — resume download, email, LinkedIn, and forward-looking note

## Interaction Scope

V1 is static-first and read-only. Optional interactions include native scrolling,
scroll-linked visual choreography, labeled node navigation, hover/focus details, and
resume download. V1 excludes accounts, comments, databases, user-generated content,
and required analytics.

No visual effect may gate access to content.
